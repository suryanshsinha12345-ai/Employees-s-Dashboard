# 👥 Employee Analytics Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Employee Analytics Dashboard** designed to analyze workforce demographics, compensation, employee distribution, and organizational trends.

The dashboard transforms employee-level data into meaningful visual insights that can help HR and business teams understand workforce composition, salary patterns, job-role distribution, employee trends, and geographic presence.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective of this project is to provide a centralized view of employee data and answer key HR-related questions such as:

- How many employees are currently working?
- What is the median annual salary?
- What is the employee exit rate?
- What is the gender distribution across the workforce?
- Which job roles have the highest number of employees?
- How has the employee record count changed over time?
- How are employees distributed across different specialties?
- Which countries contribute to the employee population?

## 📈 Dashboard Features

### 1. Employee Overview
Provides a high-level summary of the organization's workforce through key KPIs such as total employees, working employees, median salary, and exit rate.

### 2. Salary Analysis
Displays employee names along with their annual salaries, allowing users to identify employees with higher salary levels and explore compensation patterns.

### 3. Job Role Distribution
Visualizes employee counts across different job roles, including:

- Manager
- Vice President
- Senior Manager
- Director
- Analyst
- Senior Analyst
- Analyst II
- Cloud Infrastructure Architect
- Account Representative
- Other roles

### 4. Gender Distribution
The dashboard presents the workforce gender split:

- Female: 51.5%
- Male: 48.5%

This provides a quick view of gender representation within the organization.

### 5. Employee Trend Analysis
The line chart tracks employee record counts over time, helping identify changes in workforce records from the 1990s through 2021.

### 6. Specialty / Business Area Analysis
Employees are segmented across different business areas, including:

- Specialty Products
- Research & Development
- Corporate
- Manufacturing

### 7. Geographic Analysis
A geographic map provides a visual representation of employee records by country, helping identify the organization's geographical distribution.

### 8. Interactive Filtering
The dashboard includes filters for:

- Department
- Age

These filters allow users to drill down into specific employee segments and perform more focused analysis.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

Some observations from the dashboard include:

- The dataset contains **1,000 employee records**, with **897 employees currently working**.
- The reported **median salary is 92,806**.
- The employee **exit rate is 10.30%**.
- Female employees represent **51.5%** of the workforce, compared with **48.5% male employees**.
- "Others" represents the largest combined job-role category, followed by Manager and Vice President roles.
- The employee record trend generally increases over time, with noticeably higher values toward the later years.
- Corporate accounts for **254 records**, while Manufacturing accounts for **226 records**.
